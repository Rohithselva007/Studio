About downloading: your browser speaks text using its built-in voices rather than generating an audio file directly, so exporting works by recording that playback. Press a download button, then choose “This Tab” and make sure Share tab audio is checked in the permission prompt. WAV and MP3 are converted from that recording right in the browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Batch export reuses one recording permission for every track in the queue. Works most reliably in Chrome/Edge on desktop. Every export is also kept in this browser's local storage (see Saved downloads below) so you can replay or re-download it later without recording again — this stays on your device and clears if you clear site data.
